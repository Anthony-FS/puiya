# Puiya (ปุ๋ยยา)

A free, offline-first point-of-sale and compliance app for Thailand's farm-input shops
(ร้านปุ๋ย-ยา) — fertiliser, pesticide and seed retailers.

- **For shops:** fast barcode sales that work offline, stock and expiry tracking, a credit ledger
  for farmers who pay at harvest, and an automatic check that every product sold is legally
  registered with the Department of Agriculture.
- **For farmers:** digital receipts and usage instructions over LINE — no app to install.
- **For the business:** aggregated, anonymised sell-out data by district, product and week.

## Status

Foundation only: tooling, rules and the shared package. Apps are added feature by feature.
